# Project: Global Salary Analytics & Strategic Forecasting
## 1. Project Objective
The goal of this project is to analyze global labor market data to identify key salary drivers and develop a predictive framework for long-term income forecasting and strategic career planning.
## 2.Descriptive Analytics (What Happened?)
In this initial phase, we established a baseline understanding of the current market landscape.
+ Geographical Dominance: The USA and Canada consistently lead in average compensation across most job categories.
+ High-Value Roles: Specialized positions in AI and Machine Learning represent the market's upper echelon, with entry-level salaries frequently exceeding $190,000.
+ Work Modality: Remote work models consistently offer higher median salaries compared to on-site or hybrid roles across all experience levels.

## 3. Diagnostic Analytics (Why Did It Happen?)
We conducted a deep dive into the underlying variables to isolate the primary drivers of salary variance.
+ Market Volatility: The USA exhibits the highest salary spread ($38,089), indicating a highly competitive market with significant disparities between entry-level and peak compensation.
+ Seniority Premium: In sectors like Finance and Media, extensive experience (16+ years) yields a 34% salary premium, whereas entry-level compensation remains relatively uniform across various industries.
## 4. Predictive Analytics (What Will Happen?)
By applying mathematical modeling to historical data, we developed a forecasting tool for future earnings.
+ Annual Growth Coefficient: The analysis identified a reliable growth factor of $2,715.35 per year of additional experience.
+ Revenue Projections: Based on current trends, a remote professional in Canada with 10 years of experience is projected to reach an annual salary of approximately $198,801.13.
## 5. Prescriptive Analytics (What Should We Do?)
This final phase provides data-driven strategic recommendations for career optimization.
+ Strategic Targeting: To maximize ROI (Return on Investment), focus should be directed toward High-Priority segments: US/Canada-based Remote roles within the Technology and Finance sectors.
+ Decision-Support Logic: We implemented a SQL-based recommendation engine using logic-based classification to automatically identify high-value career opportunities based on salary thresholds.
## 6. Conclusion
This project demonstrates that a data-centric approach enables professionals to move beyond observation and actively engineer their career trajectory by mitigating risks and capitalizing on high-growth market opportunities.
